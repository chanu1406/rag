# RTX 4060 Technical Specifications

## Overview
The NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4060 is a mid-range graphics card released in June 2023. It is built on the Ada Lovelace architecture and features 8GB of GDDR6 memory.

## Key Specifications
- **GPU Architecture**: Ada Lovelace (AD107)
- **CUDA Cores**: 3,072
- **Memory**: 8GB GDDR6
- **Memory Bus**: 128-bit
- **Memory Bandwidth**: 272 GB/s
- **Base Clock**: 1830 MHz
- **Boost Clock**: 2460 MHz
- **TDP**: 115W
- **Process Node**: TSMC 4nm

## Performance
The RTX 4060 delivers excellent 1080p gaming performance with DLSS 3 support. It typically achieves 60+ FPS in modern AAA games at high settings.

## AI and ML Workloads
With 8GB of VRAM and support for FP8 operations, the RTX 4060 is suitable for:
- Running small to medium language models (7B parameters with quantization)
- Local stable diffusion image generation
- Video encoding with AV1 support
- RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) systems with careful VRAM management

## Power Requirements
Requires a 450W power supply with an 8-pin PCIe connector.
